# Privacy Policy

## 1. Information We Collect

### Directly Provided Information
- User IDs (randomly generated)
- Messages sent during chats
- Report submissions
- Browser/device information (via HTTP headers)

### Automatically Collected Information
- IP addresses
- Connection logs
- Error reports
- Server logs
- Timestamps of sessions

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- Real names
- Email addresses
- Physical locations
- Personal identification
- Financial information

## 2. How We Use Information

- **Service Operation**: Matching users, routing messages, maintaining connections
- **Safety & Security**: Detecting abuse, investigating reports, preventing fraud
- **Analytics**: Understanding usage patterns, improving performance
- **Legal**: Complying with laws, responding to legal requests

## 3. Data Retention

- **Session Data**: Deleted after 30 days of inactivity
- **Chat Messages**: Deleted when room closes or after 90 days
- **Reports**: Retained for 1 year for investigation purposes
- **Logs**: Retained for 30 days for debugging

## 4. Data Security

We implement:
- Secure socket connections (HTTPS/WSS)
- Encryption in transit
- Access controls
- Regular security audits

However, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Our application uses:
- **PeerJS** - WebRTC signaling (see their privacy policy)
- **Cloud providers** - For hosting

We do not sell or share user data with third parties except as required by law.

## 6. User Rights

You have the right to:
- Know what data we hold
- Request data deletion
- Opt-out of analytics
- File privacy complaints

## 7. Children

This service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 8. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@example.com

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use implies acceptance.
